Share my screen

This sends your Windows laptop screen into the laptop panel in the Quest room. Type the short code shown on that panel, then pick a screen or window.

Waiting for the code from your headset.
  1. On the Quest, open the room and read the short code on the laptop (or on the corner card before you enter VR).
  2. Type that code here, click Share my screen, and pick a window or the whole screen.
  3. Leave this tab open and keep the laptop awake. To stop, click Stop sharing or close this tab.

Room: webxr-room. Same Wi-Fi as the headset works best.
