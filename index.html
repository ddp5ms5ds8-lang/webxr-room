Two overhead screens above a glass desk, with a starfield drop beyond the edge. A laptop on the desk can be grabbed and moved, and can mirror your Windows laptop.
Laptop code will appear here.
Desktop: drag to look, WASD to walk.
Quest: Enter VR, left thumbstick forward to teleport (desk or fire). Right-hand laser + trigger plays or pauses a screen, or enlarges the laptop. Point at the laptop and squeeze either grip, move it, then let go to leave it floating.
